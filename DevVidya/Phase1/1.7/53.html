👉 Fake Website kaise pehchane? (Beginner Friendly Guide)

🔹 Real Life Example (Hindi me)

Maan lo tum market me ja rahe ho aur do shops same products bech rahe hai, ek genuine aur ek duplicate. Fake shop me jaoge to paisa waste hoga aur product safe nahi hoga. Bilkul isi tarah, internet par fake websites se bachna important hai jisse data aur money safe rahe.

🔹 Definition

Fake website wo website hoti hai jo asli website ka duplicate ya look-alike banakar users ko fool karti hai. Ye personal information, login credentials, credit/debit card info ya sensitive data chura sakti hai. Safe browsing ke liye fake websites ko pehchanna zaruri hai.

👉 Simple words me: Fake website = fraud website jo asli dikhkar data ya paisa chura sakti hai.

🔹 Signs of Fake Websites

  • URL me spelling mistakes ya unusual characters
  • HTTPS ka missing ya insecure connection
  • Pop-up ads aur excessive banners
  • Low-quality graphics aur design
  • Too good to be true offers
  • Contact information missing ya suspicious
  • Grammar aur spelling mistakes content me

🔹 How to Verify a Website

  1. Check URL carefully (example: www.amazon.com vs www.amaz0n.com)
  2. Look for HTTPS aur padlock symbol
  3. Verify company contact details aur About Us page
  4. Search online reviews aur ratings
  5. Check domain registration using WHOIS tools
  6. Test small transactions ya avoid sharing sensitive info
  7. Use antivirus browser plugins for warnings

🔹 Tools to Detect Fake Websites

  • Browser security plugins → Norton Safe Web, McAfee WebAdvisor
  • Online tools → VirusTotal URL checker
  • Google Safe Browsing → check website safety
  • Password manager alerts → detect phishing websites
  • Security apps on mobile → detect unsafe apps and links

🔹 Tips to Avoid Fake Websites

  • Always type URL manually, don’t click on random links
  • Avoid emails from unknown senders with links
  • Never share personal info on unverified websites
  • Check website reviews before login or payment
  • Update browser and antivirus regularly
  • Enable phishing & malware protection in browser settings
  • Use strong passwords and 2FA for online accounts

🔹 Advantages of Detecting Fake Websites

  • Data aur personal information safe rahega
  • Money loss avoid hoga
  • System virus-free aur secure rahega
  • Unauthorized access aur hacking prevent hoga
  • Online transactions aur shopping secure rahenge

🔹 Real Life Use Cases

  • Online banking → Avoid phishing websites for account safety
  • E-commerce → Verify trusted shopping sites before payment
  • Social media → Prevent fake login pages
  • Emails → Avoid spam and malicious links
  • Government portals → Ensure official websites for services
