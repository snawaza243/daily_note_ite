👉 Computer kaha-kaha use hota hai? (Beginner Friendly Guide)

🔹 Real Life Example (Hindi me)

Maan lo tum subah uthte ho aur apna mobile check karte ho — ye bhi ek computer hai. School jaate ho, wahan attendance aur result computer se banta hai. Ghar aakar YouTube dekhte ho — wo bhi computer ki wajah se possible hai.

Matlab simple baat: Computer har jagah use ho raha hai!

🔹 Computer kaha-kaha use hota hai?

Aaj ke digital zamane me computer almost har field me use hota hai. Chalo ek-ek karke simple tarike se samajhte hain:

🏫 1. School aur College me

  • Online classes lene ke liye
  • Result banane ke liye
  • Projects aur presentations banane ke liye
  • Computer lab me practice ke liye

🏦 2. Bank me

  • Account manage karne ke liye
  • Money transfer (online banking)
  • ATM machine chalane ke liye
  • Transaction record rakhne ke liye

🏥 3. Hospital me

  • Patient records store karne ke liye
  • Reports banane ke liye
  • X-ray aur scanning me
  • Online appointment system

🏢 4. Office me

  • Data entry aur reports banane ke liye
  • Email bhejne ke liye
  • Meetings aur presentations ke liye
  • Company management ke liye

🏬 5. Shops aur Business me

  • Billing aur invoice banane ke liye
  • Stock manage karne ke liye
  • Online selling (E-commerce)

🎮 6. Entertainment (Manoranjan) me

  • Movies dekhna
  • Games khelna
  • Music sunna
  • YouTube aur OTT platforms

🚗 7. Transport aur Travel me

  • Train aur flight ticket booking
  • GPS navigation (Google Maps)
  • Traffic control systems

🌐 8. Internet aur Communication me

  • Email bhejna
  • Video calling (Zoom, Meet)
  • Social media (WhatsApp, Instagram)

🔬 9. Science aur Research me

  • Data analysis
  • Space research
  • New inventions aur experiments

🏠 10. Daily Life me

  • Online shopping
  • Bill payment
  • Online classes
  • Work from home

👉 Aaj ke time me hum bina computer ke life imagine bhi nahi kar sakte.
