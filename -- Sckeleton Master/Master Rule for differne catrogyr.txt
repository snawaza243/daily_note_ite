🧩 MASTER RULE (Sab Category ke liye Same Base)

Har post me ye 5 cheeze MUST:

✅ Introduction
✅ Structured headings
✅ Useful content
✅ FAQ
✅ Conclusion

✅ 1️⃣ TECH / HOW-TO / GUIDE BLOG SKELETON

(Software, Online Service, Blogging, Windows Fix)

Title: How to [Solve / Do Something] – Complete Guide

1. Introduction
2. What is [Topic]
3. Why it is Used
4. Requirements
5. Step-by-Step Guide
6. Screenshots Explanation
7. Common Errors & Fix
8. Pro Tips
9. FAQ
10. Conclusion

✅ Best for:

Tech

Online earning

Software

Government services
